# Personal Loan Underwriting Guidelines

Company policy for unsecured personal loans. Underwriters and automated checks must apply every rule below.

## Eligibility age

Applicants must be at least 21 years old and not older than 65 at the time of application. Applications outside this age range are declined.

## Employment and work requirements

Accepted employment statuses: employed, self-employed, and contract. Unemployed and student applicants are not eligible for personal loans under this policy.

Work tenure rules (must be met for the loan amount requested):

- Employed or contract: at least 6 months continuous work with the current employer for loans up to 200,000; at least 12 months for loans above 200,000.
- Self-employed: at least 12 months continuous business operation for loans up to 200,000; at least 24 months for loans above 200,000.
- Applicants who cannot meet the tenure rule for the requested loan amount must be declined or asked to reduce the loan amount to the band they qualify for.

## Minimum salary and income capacity vs loan amount

Absolute income floor: monthly income must be at least 20,000. Applicants below this floor are not eligible regardless of credit score.

Income must also fit the loan value. Use annual income = monthlyIncome x 12:

- Loan amount up to 100,000: annual income must be at least 240,000 (monthly income at least 20,000).
- Loan amount 100,001 to 250,000: annual income must be at least 480,000 (monthly income at least 40,000).
- Loan amount 250,001 to 500,000: annual income must be at least 720,000 (monthly income at least 60,000).

If monthly income is below the band required for the requested loan amount, decline or require a lower loan amount that matches the applicant's income band.

## Credit score bands

- Credit score below 580: decline.
- Credit score 580 to 649: may approve only if debt-to-income is under 30% and loan amount is at most 150,000.
- Credit score 650 to 719: standard approval path if other rules pass.
- Credit score 720 and above: preferred pricing path if other rules pass.

## Debt-to-income (DTI)

Compute DTI as (existingDebt + loanAmount) / (monthlyIncome * 12). Maximum allowed DTI is 40%. If DTI exceeds 40%, decline the application. DTI is an additional check on top of the income-capacity bands above.

## Maximum loan amount and term

Maximum loan amount is 500,000. Loan term must be between 12 and 60 months inclusive. Amounts above 500,000 or terms outside 12–60 months are declined.

Estimated monthly installment should not exceed 40% of monthly income. Approximate installment as loanAmount / loanTermMonths. If installment exceeds 40% of monthlyIncome, decline.

## Summary decision order

1. Confirm age and employment status.
2. Confirm work tenure matches the requested loan amount.
3. Confirm absolute income floor and income-capacity band for the loan amount.
4. Confirm amount, term, and installment affordability.
5. Confirm credit score band rules.
6. Confirm DTI is within 40%.
7. Approve only when all checks pass; otherwise decline and cite the failed rule.
